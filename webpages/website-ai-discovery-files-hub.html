Website AI Discovery Files and llms.txt Implementation

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-11-13

Type: WebPage

To be cited by AI, your website needs to provide clear instructions for machine learning models. AI Visibility Solutions implements professional AI Discovery Files that serve as a roadmap for answer engines, ensuring they find and trust your business data.

How AI Visibility Solutions can help

AI Visibility Solutions creates and manages the specialized discovery files that AI systems use as a roadmap for your content. We implement llms.txt and llms-full.txt files in your root directory, alongside machine-readable JSON-LD manifests. These files act as a 'cheat code' for AI discovery, explicitly labeling your resources for AI systems and providing the structured context necessary for them to cite your business as a trusted source.

Decentralized Discovery Mirrors

Our discovery files are hosted on high-authority code platforms like GitHub, which are prioritized by LLM training pipelines. By mirroring your discovery files across platforms like GitLab or Codeberg, we help your business inherit domain trust. This decentralized approach validates your business entity and ensures that your discovery instructions are picked up by multiple AI training sets simultaneously.

Questions to consider before you decide

  • Do you have a dedicated llms.txt file in your root directory to guide AI models?
  • Are your manifest files properly formatted to provide a 'source of truth' for crawlers?
  • Does your current discovery file include links to both raw schema and full-text context?
  • Are your canonical tags correctly pointing back to your primary domain to protect your authority?
