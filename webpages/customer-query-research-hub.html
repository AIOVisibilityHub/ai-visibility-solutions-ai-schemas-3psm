Customer Query Research

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-11-13

Type: WebPage

Modern search is conversational, yet most businesses still target isolated keywords. AI Visibility Solutions researches the actual questions your customers are asking across the web to ensure your AI content strategy is built on real demand.

How AI Visibility Solutions can help

AI Visibility Solutions performs deep research to identify the exact questions prospects ask when they aren't searching for a business by name. We move beyond keyword tools to uncover real human inquiries on platforms like Reddit, Quora, and industry-specific Q&A sites. By identifying 'unanswered questions' in your search data, we help you fill information gaps that AI engines are actively looking to satisfy with authoritative citations.

Cross-Platform Intent Discovery

Our research process utilizes the Perplexity API deep research mode to scan high-intent platforms. For law firms, we analyze queries on Avvo and Justia; for contractors and home services, we research Houzz, Yelp Q&A, and Google Business Profile Q&A. This identifies high-intent questions tied to specific service scenarios, such as 'emergency plumber near me' or specific legal case types, which are then used to build your AI Resource Library.

Service Unbundling for Answer Engines

We translate a standard service list—which might only have 10-20 items—into hundreds of individual topics based on how people actually search for help. This 'unbundling' of services ensures that every specific scenario that could lead to a project estimate is covered by a dedicated answer, providing the granularity that LLMs require to recommend your business for complex or conversational prompts.

Questions to consider before you decide

  • What 'unanswered questions' are currently appearing in your Google Search Console?
  • Are you tracking conversational queries on platforms like Reddit, Quora, or Avvo?
  • Do your current service pages address the specific 'near me' scenarios your customers face?
  • How does your service list translate into the natural language people use when speaking to a phone?
