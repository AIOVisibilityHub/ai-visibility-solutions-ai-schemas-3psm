Law Firm AI Visibility Audit

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-11-13

Type: WebPage

Before you can be cited by AI, you need to know if AI can even 'see' your firm. A Law Firm AI Audit is the first step in moving from traditional SEO to Answer Engine Optimization.

How AI Visibility Solutions can help

An AI Visibility Solutions audit for law firms identifies exactly why your practice isn't showing up in Google AI Overviews or ChatGPT. We analyze your site’s technical crawlability, the 'fact density' of your legal content, and the health of your schema markup. The result is a roadmap to turning your legal expertise into a machine-readable knowledge base that answer engines trust.

Technical AI Crawlability Assessment

Our audit specifically looks for the 'AI Visibility Gap.' Many law firm sites are built on heavy CMS themes (like WordPress) that are slow for AI crawlers to parse. We evaluate your site’s HTML to see if it provides the low-latency, clean data that AI re-rankers prefer. We also check for the presence of AI discovery files (like llms.txt) in your root directory, which are essential for guiding modern LLM bots to your most important legal content.

Entity & Content Density Evaluation

We analyze your current FAQ and practice area content against real-world query data from platforms like Avvo and Reddit. The audit identifies 'unanswered questions' where your firm could be the primary authority. We evaluate your current schema implementation (LegalService, Attorney, FAQPage) to ensure it is triple-nested and correctly defines your firm as a trusted entity. This ensures your data is ready to be published to a decentralized authority network.

Questions to consider before you decide

  • Does your current site have render-blocking JavaScript that stops AI crawlers?
  • Are your practice area pages missing deep JSON-LD schema?
  • Is your legal content structured with question-based headings for AEO?
  • Do you have a manifest of all your legal entities (attorneys, locations, services) for AI to consume?
