AEO for Small Business

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-11-13

Type: WebPage

For small businesses, appearing in AI Overviews is the new local search frontier. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the specialized process of making your business the default answer when prospects ask AI assistants direct questions.

How AI Visibility Solutions can help

We turn your local business expertise into a machine-readable knowledge base. By researching the specific, high-intent questions your prospects ask on platforms like Quora, Reddit, and Yelp, we build a static HTML 'AI Resource Library' that gives answer engines the structured, low-latency data they prefer for citations.

Answer-First Research Methodology

Our AEO process begins by identifying 'unanswered questions' in your Google Search Console and scraping platforms like Avvo, Houzz, and Reddit for real-world queries tied to your specific services. We look for 'near me' scenarios and specific service gaps (e.g., 'emergency plumber near me' or 'slip and fall lawyer') that competitors often ignore, providing the exact answers prospects are seeking in conversational search.

Content Architected for AI & Voice Pickup

Every page in our AI Resource Library is architected for two outcomes: typed AI search and voice assistants. We use 'Question-based headings' followed immediately by a direct answer to facilitate AI extraction. Furthermore, we apply 'Speakable Schema' to every article, telling voice assistants exactly which sentence to read aloud, which is critical for the 'one-answer' stakes of Siri and Alexa queries.

Questions to consider before you decide

  • Are you targeting 'unanswered questions' from your Google Search Console?
  • Is your FAQ content structured with direct answers at the top of each section?
  • Are your technical files (llms.txt) properly placed in your root directory?
  • Do you have a plan for continuous monthly expansion of your AI Resource Library?
