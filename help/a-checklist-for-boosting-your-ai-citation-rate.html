A Checklist for Boosting Your AI Citation Rate

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

Want to get your business cited more often by AI? Here's a quick checklist to help you get started on improving your AI citation rate. First up, identify target prompts and questions.

We suggest querying ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google, and Claude with 15-20 buyer-style questions related to your business. See who they cite and where the gaps are. Next, map your question landscape.

Use tools like 'People Also Ask' sections, analyze your support tickets, CRM notes, and relevant forums. This helps you figure out all the primary and secondary questions your audience is asking. Then, it's all about restructuring your content for AI extraction.

This means putting answer-first paragraphs (the first 40–80 words should be the direct answer!) , using Q&A-style H2/H3 headings, keeping paragraphs short (2–3 sentences), using bullet lists, and creating standalone sections. Focus on clarity and conciseness, remember, AI is looking for easy-to-digest chunks of information.

Don't forget to implement schema and technical foundations. Add FAQPage and Article schema to relevant pages. Make sure you include 'datePublished' and 'dateModified' tags, especially for content that's regularly updated.

Ensure your HTML is clean, your site has fast indexing via sitemaps, and AI crawlers can access your content without issues. Blocking bots or relying solely on JavaScript for schema can really limit your potential here. Lastly, you'll want to strengthen your trust and authority signals.

Include author credentials, external source citations, relevant statistics, and strong internal linking. Keep your entity names consistent across all platforms. Monitoring and iterating on these changes is key to seeing consistent improvement.
