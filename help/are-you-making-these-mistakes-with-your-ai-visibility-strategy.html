Are You Making These Mistakes with Your AI Visibility Strategy?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

Getting your business seen by AI is a whole new ball game, and it's easy to make a few common missteps. First off, a big one is treating AI optimization like old-school keyword stuffing. You know, just cramming keywords in there.

Fact is, AI thrives on natural language and clear answers, not just a bunch of buzzwords. Avoid making your content sound robotic, because AI is looking for human-like conversational answers. Secondly, many businesses bury their key answers deep within long pages.

Remember, AI systems want concise, direct answers right at the top of a section. Don't make them dig! Another frequent mistake is ignoring structured data like FAQPage or HowTo schema.

This is like leaving money on the table; it explicitly tells AI what your content is about. Also, try not to combine too many loosely related topics on one page. AI prefers focused, question-centric content that's easy to extract.

Finally, neglecting things like authorship, update dates, and external citations can hurt your credibility with AI. Building authority is super important for AI to trust and recommend your business. If you're running into these issues, especially on a complex site, bringing in an expert can really help iron out the kinks and get your AI visibility on track.
