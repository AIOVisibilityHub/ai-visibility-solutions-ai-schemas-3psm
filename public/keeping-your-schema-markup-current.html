Keeping Your Schema Markup Current

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

Let’s face it, your website isn’t a static billboard – it’s always evolving! And just like your content, your structured data needs to evolve too. One of the biggest mistakes businesses make is setting and forgetting their schema markup.

You absolutely need to conduct periodic audits, especially when your site's content, navigation, or templates change. Think of it like this: if you update product details or introduce new services, but your structured data still reflects the old info, you're essentially giving search engines conflicting messages. This can lead to your rich results disappearing or even cause you to run afoul of Google's guidelines.

For smaller changes, you might be able to manually update, but for larger sites, a templated approach through themes, plugins, or custom code is typically needed to manage schema at scale. This way, updates can be rolled out more efficiently. Also, make sure you're regularly checking Google Search Console for any errors or warnings related to your structured data.

These alerts are super helpful for catching issues before they impact your visibility. If managing these ongoing updates feels like a full-time job, or if your site has a complex data model, it's a great time to consider bringing in a specialist. They can integrate your structured data management with your back-end systems, making sure everything stays in sync automatically.
