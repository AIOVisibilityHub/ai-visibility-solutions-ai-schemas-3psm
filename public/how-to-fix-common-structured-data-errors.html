How to Fix Common Structured Data Errors

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Team Members

Structured data is awesome for AI visibility, but errors can definitely throw a wrench in the gears. The good news is, many common issues are fixable! One big mistake is using FAQ schema for non-FAQ content or where the answers aren't single and clear.

Always make sure your question and answer pairs are genuine and directly related to the page's topic. Another frequent error is writing answers that are too long or don't align with the content on the page itself. Keep it concise and relevant!

And seriously, never skip the validation step. Tools are out there to help you test your markup before or right after deployment. Google Search Console is your best friend here; check it regularly for errors and warnings.

You'll want to correct those invalid items as soon as you spot them. If you're seeing a bunch of errors, especially across multiple pages, or if debugging feels like trying to solve a Rubik's Cube blindfolded, that's a sign it might be time to bring in the pros. They can help you sort through complex schema issues, ensuring your data is clean, compliant, and ready for AI to use effectively.
